About the Club

Located in the northern port city of Hamburg, Hamburger SV (HSV) is one of Germany’s oldest, most historic, and most passionately supported football clubs.

The Stadium

HSV plays its home matches at the Volksparkstadion, a stadium with a capacity of 57,000 spectators. The stadium has undergone several renovations and is known for its modern facilities and vibrant atmosphere during match days.

The stadium is located within the Volkspark, a large public park in Hamburg, and is easily accessible by public transportation. The stadium has hosted numerous international matches and events, making it a significant landmark in the city.
