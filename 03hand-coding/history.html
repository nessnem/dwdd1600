History

Founded in 1887 HSV is one of Germany's oldest and most historical football clubs becoming German champions 6 times, German cup winners 3 times and even lifting the highly coveted European Cup in 1983.

Early Years

Hamburger SV (HSV) traces its formal roots back to September 29, 1887, through the founding of its oldest predecessor, SC Germania Hamburg. However, the modern version of the club was officially established on June 2, 1919, following a crucial post-World War I merger between:

  1. SC Germania
  2. Hamburger FC
  3. FC Falke 06

The merger paid off almost instantly, turning Hamburg into a national powerhouse during the Weimar Republic era.
