Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include

John Hancock
As president of the Second Continental Congress, he wrote his name in large, bold letters so King George III could read it without glasses. His name became a slang term for a signature.
Benjamin Franklin
Representing Pennsylvania at age 70, he was the oldest signer of the document and a famous scientist, inventor, and diplomat.
Thomas Jefferson
A delegate from Virginia, he wrote the original draft of the Declaration and later became the third U.S. President.
John Adams
A fiery leader and lawyer from Massachusetts, he helped push the colonies to vote for freedom and later became the second U.S. President.
Roger Sherman
A statesman from Connecticut, he is the only person who signed all four major U.S. founding documents, including the Constitution.
